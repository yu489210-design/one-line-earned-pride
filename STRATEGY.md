# Strategy

One line, 1 to 240 characters. Judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, no ties)
weighs, in order: (1) genuine earned pride, a quiet moment for someone in a
scene, a small person doing something hard or a long effort finally landing;
(2) a concrete scene with a real person, seen happening rather than
summarised; (3) pride earned inside the line by effort or difficulty the
reader can feel, never asserted, congratulated or explained; (4) it lands in
one read, pride arriving by the last word, no setup to assemble; (5)
freshness: not the stock trophy, podium or graduation.

Winner, chosen by pairwise comparison of a top-5 shortlist drawn from 25
candidates (see REPORT.md):

"The boy split the last log before dark, stacked it straight, and his grandfather nodded once and handed him the axe to keep."

Why: the effort is felt in the body (split, stacked, before dark), the scene
is one concrete yard at dusk, and the pride is never named — it arrives in
the last four words, when the grandfather hands the axe over to keep. The
axe changing hands is a transfer of trust, not a prize: quiet, permanent,
and earned. 124 characters, one line, no "proud", no trophy, no podium, no
graduation, no applause.

Avoided saturated angles in this bounty: trophy / podium / graduation /
marathon finish line, "she was so proud", "he finally did it", applause and
standing ovations, generic "after years of hard work" summaries with no
scene, and any line that explains the pride instead of staging it.
